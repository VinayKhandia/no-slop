# Minimalism style recipe

Best for: premium products, high-end SaaS, luxury architecture, minimalist studios.

## Palette family
Near-monochromatic base, massive tonal restraint, quiet whisper accent. Adapt, do not copy:
- Alabaster #F8F7F4, charcoal #1A1A18, warm stone #E5E2DC, muted olive #5E6354
- Chalk #F5F5F7, graphite #141517, cool fog #DFE1E5, soft bronze #8C7B6B
- Bone #F3EFEA, pitch #111111, linen #D9D2C7, slate accent #4A5568

## Type
Refined, understated grotesk or subtle serif (Inter Tight, Syne, Cormorant Garamond, Newsreader). Generous letter-spacing on small caps, line-height 1.1 on single large headlines (8 to 11vw). Minimal weights used: only regular and light.

## Layout
Massive open breathing room (60%+ empty canvas), single focal concept per section, strictly one action or statement per screen. No competing sidebars or dense cards.

## Motion
Slow, meditative eases (duration 1.2 to 1.6s, power2.out), whisper fades, subtle 4% scale transitions, quiet scroll opacity fades without flashy bounces.

## 3D environment
Empty monolithic void or soft diffused studio (#F0EDE8) with an infinite floor horizon, ambient occlusion, one ultra-soft key light casting a long faint shadow.

## Cursor
Subtle hollow circle (16px) that expands into a 24px soft ring on clickable elements with gentle lerp.

## Copy tone
Essential, quiet, confident. Understated single sentences. Zero filler copy.

## Avoid
Dense grids, loud accents, cluttered badges, multi-layer cards, busy particles, bouncy springs.
