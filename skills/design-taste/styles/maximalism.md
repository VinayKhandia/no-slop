# Maximalism style recipe

Best for: music festivals, creative studios, streetwear, entertainment, counter-culture brands.

## Palette family
Hyper-vibrant clashing tones, saturated primaries and neon contrasts. Adapt, do not copy:
- Acid lemon #E5FF00, electric cobalt #1D00FF, hot magenta #FF0055, midnight #080811
- Toxic green #00FF66, ultraviolet #7928CA, flame orange #FF5500, deep plum #150A21
- Canary #FFE600, turquoise #00F0FF, candy pink #FF1493, onyx #0B0B0C

## Type
Expressive, high-energy clash of hyper-bold display and condensed sans (Clash Display, Bebas Neue, Bungee, Space Grotesk). Giant 14 to 18vw display headlines, overlapping lines, mixed weights, angled text.

## Layout
Controlled chaos: multi-layered collage, overlapping graphic planes, diagonal ribbons, dense sticker clusters, full-bleed textured backgrounds, zero empty gutters.

## Motion
Velocity-skewed marquees, aggressive magnetic pulls, snappy bouncy springs (elastic.out), fast card rotations on hover (+-10deg), abrupt color wipe section transitions.

## 3D environment
Dense saturated room with clashing light sources (cyan key, pink rim light), floating confetti shards or geometric orbiting satellites, dynamic camera tilts with scroll speed.

## Cursor
Large inverted circular lens or spinning star icon that shifts colors when passing over text blocks.

## Copy tone
Unapologetic, punchy, visceral. Loud exclamations, punchy slogans, conversational swagger.

## Avoid
Pale washed-out palettes, excessive sterile whitespace, muted corporate copy, timid subtle fades.
