# Dark luxe style recipe

Best for: luxury watches, exotic automotive, ultra-premium spirits, exclusive nightlife, private wealth.

## Palette family
Deep velvety black depths, subtle charcoal surfaces, precious metal highlights. Adapt, do not copy:
- Obsidian velvet #0A0A0C, smoked charcoal #16161A, brushed champagne #D4AF37, mist #9E9E9E
- Onyx black #08080A, slate shadow #141519, polished copper #C87D55, platinum #E5E5EA
- Caviar dark #0D0E11, graphite #1A1C22, burnished rose gold #B76E79, silver #C0C0C8

## Type
Refined aristocratic serif or razor-sharp luxury grotesque (Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Italiana, Syne). Elegant high-contrast tracking, 9 to 13vw hero display, understated uppercase subtitles.

## Layout
Cinematic wide-ratio viewports, expansive dark negative space, edge-to-edge cinematic imagery, razor-thin hairline borders (rgba(212,175,55,0.2)), deliberate centered symmetry.

## Motion
Silky, cinematic slow reveals (duration 1.2s to 1.8s): smooth light reflections gliding across surfaces, gentle camera dolly on scroll, slow gold shimmer underlines, slow fade-up typography.

## 3D environment
Moody dark room with dramatic studio lighting: three-point lighting with sharp golden rim light, deep dark shadows, glossy reflective floor plane with high roughness falloff, atmospheric low-density fog.

## Cursor
Refined 3px platinum dot with an ultra-thin 28px outer gold ring following with smooth inertia.

## Copy tone
Exclusive, understated, evocative. Speaks to heritage, precision, rarity, and timeless craft.

## Avoid
Cheap bright rainbow gradients, bouncy playful springs, bubbly rounded containers, loud discount badges.
