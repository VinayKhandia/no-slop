# Editorial style recipe

Best for: cafes, restaurants, magazines, fashion, portfolios, hospitality, culture.

## Palette family
Paper or tinted backgrounds, ink text, one bold spot color. Adapt, do not copy:
- Paper #EFE7DA, ink #1A1614, tomato #D8452B, sage #9FB49A
- Oat #E9DFCF, ink #231A15, deep green #1F4D3A, butter #F2C94C
- Blush #F3DCD4, ink #2A1F1D, cherry #B3202A

## Type
High-contrast serif display (Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Playfair Display, DM Serif Display) plus a clean sans or mono for captions (DM Sans, Manrope, Space Mono). Headlines 10 to 14vw, line-height 0.9. Tiny uppercase captions, folios, issue numbers.

## Layout
12-column grid, wide margins, hairline rules, numbered chapters, pull quotes, asymmetric text and image blocks, big crops, captions under visuals. The object sits like a magazine cover subject.

## Motion
Line-mask headline reveals, word-by-word scrub, slow image parallax, sticky chapter numbers, marquee between chapters, hover image previews on lists, color wipe between chapters.

## 3D environment
A tinted color room (one saturated wall color from the palette, paper-textured floor, soft key light, soft contact shadow) that rotates slowly with scroll. Not a dark chamber.

## Cursor
Small dot with an italic serif label (View, Drag, Open).

## Copy tone
Magazine voice: short, sensory, human. Chapter titles, not specs.

## Avoid
Dark backgrounds with a glowing accent, HUD or telemetry jargon, monospace everywhere, three-card grids.
