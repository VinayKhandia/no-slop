# Y2K style recipe

Best for: youth culture, gen-z streetwear, retro tech accessories, electronic music.

## Palette family
Chrome metallics, futuristic pastels, iridescent cyber accents. Adapt, do not copy:
- Chrome silver #E0E7FF, cyber cyan #00F0FF, bubblegum #FF70A6, deep matrix #0A0E1A
- Iridescent ice #D8F3DC, electric lime #70E000, hot lilac #C77DFF, tech navy #091528
- Pearl frost #EDF2F4, liquid chrome #8D99AE, neon pink #EF476F, dark obsidian #10121A

## Type
Futuristic techno-display and pixel fonts (Orbitron, Audiowide, VT323, Silkscreen) paired with clean geometric sans (Chakra Petch, Space Grotesk). Capsule pill badges, italicized techno labels.

## Layout
Cyber-portal aesthetic: pill containers, starburst stickers, metallic bevel cards, sticker-bomb badges, holographic border accents, horizontal scrolling gadget feeds.

## Motion
Chrome reflection sweeps, springy bubble scale-ups (back.out), retro TV scanline flickers on entrance, rotating 3D star charms, bouncy floating badges.

## 3D environment
Chrome matrix chamber: reflective metallic ground plane, high-specular environment reflections, iridescent rim lighting, floating chrome rings or liquid bubbles drifting around the hero.

## Cursor
Pixelated metallic cross or 90s digital arrow with animated cyber star sparkle trail.

## Copy tone
Hyper-playful, nostalgic, energetic. Tech optimism, early web nostalgia, punchy bite-sized slang.

## Avoid
Muted brown earthy tones, vintage paper textures, conservative corporate grids, staid serif body copy.
