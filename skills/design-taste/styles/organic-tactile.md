# Organic-tactile style recipe

Best for: sustainable goods, natural wellness, craft pottery, organic food, slow living.

## Palette family
Earthy clays, warm ochres, botanical moss, soft sun-bleached linen. Adapt, do not copy:
- Warm linen #F4EFEB, terracotta #C36B4E, olive moss #5B6B48, charred umber #28211D
- Sandstone #EDE4D8, sun ochre #D49B42, eucalyptus #6E8879, dark soil #2B231F
- Clay blush #F2E3DB, rust red #A84835, sage herb #82967C, espresso #201A18

## Type
Warm humanist serifs or soft rounded grotesks (Fraunces, Recoleta, Quicksand, Plus Jakarta Sans). Organic letterforms, relaxed line-heights (1.4 to 1.6), gently rounded headlines.

## Layout
Soft asymmetrical pebble shapes, wavy edge contours, breathable whitespace, overlapping earthy photo cards with textured frames, gentle staggered columns without harsh grid lines.

## Motion
Soft fluid breathing motions, gentle floating wave undulations, slow organic bloom reveals, smooth dampening on drag, natural settling physics without rigid snaps.

## 3D environment
Warm sunlit clay studio: warm directional key light mimicking morning window light, subtle textured plaster wall, soft clay-like procedural materials with gentle roughness, soft floor contact shadow.

## Cursor
Warm terracotta smooth dot that expands into a soft organic water-drop contour on hover.

## Copy tone
Warm, mindful, grounded. Focus on provenance, ingredients, craftsmanship, and tactile sensation.

## Avoid
Cold steel grays, harsh neon accents, hard square black borders, rushed mechanical animations.
