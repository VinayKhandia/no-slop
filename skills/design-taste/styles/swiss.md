# Swiss style recipe

Best for: agencies, tools, architecture, design systems, editorial data.

## Palette family
Pure or cool white backgrounds, neutral dark text, single signal accent. Adapt, do not copy:
- White #F7F7F8, carbon #111215, signal red #E63920, cool grey #8E9299
- Pure #F4F5F6, slate #18191B, international orange #FF4F00, mist #D8DBE0
- Cream white #F9F8F5, jet #0E0F12, cobalt blue #0047FF, ash #A2A4A8

## Type
Strict sans-serif hierarchy (Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk, Archivo, Epilogue) with a structured mono (JetBrains Mono). Flush-left, rag-right, no justified blocks. Bold scale contrast: 9 to 12vw numbers alongside 13px micro labels.

## Layout
Rigid 12-column modular grid, visible hairline dividers, asymmetric column spans, huge whitespace reserves, left-aligned anchors. Content never wanders off grid lines.

## Motion
Mechanical precision: linear or sharp expo.out reveals, horizontal slide reveals, tabular counter count-ups, hairline rule draws (scaleX 0 to 1), strict stepped state transitions without overshoot.

## 3D environment
Clean architectural cyclorama: neutral matte gray or white studio (#EEEEF0), sharp geometric ground shadow, directional key light with crisp edge definition, precise 45-degree orthographic or telephoto perspective.

## Cursor
Precision hairline crosshair or small 4px square with coordinate readout label.

## Copy tone
Terse, factual, objective. Metrics, data points, and structural declarations instead of marketing hyperbole.

## Avoid
Playful illustrations, decorative gradients, rounded bubbly pills, centered body copy, skeuomorphism.
