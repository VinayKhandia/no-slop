# Neo-brutalism style recipe

Best for: modern dev startups, indie hacker tools, creator economy, irreverent brands.

## Palette family
High-contrast pop saturated fills, harsh black outlines, stark paper backgrounds. Adapt, do not copy:
- Bright canary #FFDE59, ink black #000000, coral punch #FF5757, clean canvas #FFFDF8
- Electric mint #54E38E, hard black #121212, bright lilac #B388FF, eggshell #F6F4EE
- Solar tangerine #FF9F1C, deep carbon #050505, sky cyan #2EC4B6, pale parchment #F9F7F1

## Type
Raw unpretentious grotesque and monospaced type (Space Grotesk, Syne, JetBrains Mono, Archivo Black). Thick heavy headings, all-caps pill tags, stark high-contrast body copy.

## Layout
Hard 2px to 3px solid black borders, hard unblurred drop-shadows (4px 4px 0px #000), asymmetric card grids, chunky button blocks with visible offset corners, raw table borders.

## Motion
Zero-blur physical movements: tactile button press (translate 3px 3px with shadow collapse), snappy 0.15s state swaps, marquee banners, card tilts without soft shadows.

## 3D environment
Flat-lit pop art world: low-poly or bold outlined geometric models, cel-shaded materials, hard-edged directional sun shadow without soft penumbra, solid bright color backdrop wall.

## Cursor
Chunky black pixel arrow or oversized solid circle with hard black drop-shadow.

## Copy tone
Direct, irreverent, no-bullshit. Plain-spoken developer language, raw honesty, zero fluff.

## Avoid
Blurry drop shadows, subtle pastel fades, translucent glass panels, delicate thin-serif fonts.
