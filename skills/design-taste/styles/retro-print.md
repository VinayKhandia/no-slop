# Retro-print style recipe

Best for: artisan bakeries, heritage breweries, traditional barbershops, vintage workwear.

## Palette family
Sun-faded newsprint, aged ledger parchment, distressed ink stamps. Adapt, do not copy:
- Newsprint cream #F6F0E4, worn stamp black #221F1B, vintage brick #A63A2A, faded mustard #D19C38
- Aged parchment #EFE5D0, printer ink #1C1A17, forest green #2D503E, sepia ochre #B88339
- Butcher paper #E8DCBE, charcoal #1E1B18, indigo stamp #2C3E50, heritage crimson #932924

## Type
Heritage woodtype, slab-serifs and retro display (Ultra, Playfair Display, Alfa Slab One, Courier Prime). Arch-curved badges, condensed bold headlines, typewriter-style tabular details.

## Layout
Editorial broadsheet layout: multi-column newspaper grid, boxed ornamental frames, starburst guarantee seals, engraved divider rules, stamped price tags, dense vintage catalog cards.

## Motion
Paper unfold animations, analog stamp press effects (scale down 1.05 to 1.0 with subtle rotate), slow parallax between paper layers, horizontal reel scrolling, page-flip transitions.

## 3D environment
Warm tabletop workshop or vintage letterpress studio: textured woodgrain or kraft paper ground, warm tungsten bulb overhead light with warm falloff, vintage tactile artifacts with baked ambient shadows.

## Cursor
Vintage ink stamp outline or target stamp badge that briefly scales on click.

## Copy tone
Handcrafted, nostalgic, authentic. Pride of origin, traditional recipes, established dates, honest trade guarantees.

## Avoid
Slick synthetic neon colors, glossy plastic 3D materials, high-tech HUD overlays, modern generic card stacks.
