# Surreal style recipe

Best for: experimental digital art, luxury perfume, avant-garde fashion, conceptual games.

## Palette family
Dreamy unearthly gradients, twilight tones, iridescent contrasts. Adapt, do not copy:
- Dawn lavender #DDD6FE, deep space #0F0E17, solar coral #FF6B6B, ethereal mint #A7F3D0
- Cloud silver #E2E8F0, abyssal teal #0A192F, luminous peach #FFB4A2, neon violet #8B5CF6
- Mist violet #EDE9FE, midnight navy #0D1117, pearl shimmer #FCE7F3, aurora green #34D399

## Type
Unconventional, liquid or sculptured typefaces (Cinzel Decorative, Syne, Italiana, Playfair Display) paired with airy sans (Plus Jakarta Sans). Extreme scale shifts: 12vw poetic phrases over tiny spaced tracking.

## Layout
Dreamscape composition: floating detached cards, liquid asymmetrical silhouettes, non-linear section flow, unexpected object scale (giant key, tiny doorway), broken horizons.

## Motion
Floating levitation with sin/cos hover drift, fluid distortion wipes, slow celestial camera dolly along 3D curves, dreamlike dissolve transitions between sections.

## 3D environment
Surreal landscape or endless misty ground plane: gradient fog ceiling, giant celestial orb or floating impossible geometry, two-tone pastel lighting (rose and ice-blue), gentle floating dust motes.

## Cursor
Soft shimmering orb with trailing stardust particles (max 15 points) that gently distort on drag.

## Copy tone
Poetic, evocative, philosophical. Provocative queries and sensory fragments.

## Avoid
Rigid corporate tables, predictable dashboard layouts, harsh utilitarian borders, tech telemetry jargon.
